Become a Picker tester

Picker is about to launch on Google Play. Before that, Google wants testers who keep the app for 14 days in a row. We are looking for 15 people. Will you help?

You need an Android phone with Google Play. iPhone doesn't work for this test.

How it works

  1. Sign upFill in the form below. Use the Google address you are signed in with on Google Play.
  2. Wait to be addedLuca adds you to the test on Google Play and lets you know by WhatsApp or email.
  3. Join the testOpen the link below with that same Google address, tap “Become a tester”, then “Download it on Google Play” and install Picker. Open the test on Google Play The link only works once you have been added.
  4. Keep it, then 14 daysAs soon as 15 testers are in, Luca gives the go for the 14 days. Until then, just keep Picker on your phone: it is light on battery and data, and it was built with security in mind.After the go: leave Picker installed for 14 days, open it now and then and don't leave the test.
  5. Tell us what you thinkBugs, ideas, praise: in the app under Settings → Feedback, or just send Luca a message.

Ads: only tap test ads

Picker shows a few ads. As a tester, only tap an ad if it says “Test Ad” at the top. Clicks on real ads by testers can get the developer's ad account banned. Seeing them is fine.

Find your advertising ID

With it, your phone only gets test ads. It is optional, but it helps a lot.

  • Most phones (Samsung and others): Settings → Google → All services → Ads
  • Pixel and Android 12 or later: Settings → Security & privacy → More privacy settings → Ads

Copy “Your advertising ID” and paste it below. If it only shows zeros, the ID was deleted: tap “Get new advertising ID” or leave the field empty.

What happens with your details

Luca Parise (LuTekOps), the developer of Picker, uses your name, Google address, advertising ID and mobile number only for this test: to add you as a tester on Google Play and as a test device in Google AdMob, and to reach you by WhatsApp or email. They are stored with Supabase (EU) and sent to Luca by email via Resend (USA). They are deleted 6 months after you sign up at the latest, or right away if you ask: picker-smartdecisions@lutekops.ch.
